Pishpirik – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Pishpirik is a card game for web browsers and LG webOS TVs. Playing against the computer requires no account and collects no personal data.

What we collect (online play only)

  • Username and password you choose when creating an account. The password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us.
  • Quick play without an account (“Luaj me dikë”): Firebase gives the app a random anonymous ID. Your opponent sees only a generated name such as “Lojtar 4F2K” and whether you play on a phone, computer or TV. While you search, this name and the time of the search are kept in a waiting list and removed as soon as a game starts or you cancel.
  • Friends list, game invitations, game state and results, so you can play with friends.
  • Chat messages you send during an online game (quick phrases, emoji or short text), visible to your opponent.

We do not collect your real name, email address, location, device identifiers or advertising IDs. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

How it is used

Only to provide the game: signing in, finding friends by username and playing online. Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with other companies.

Where it is stored

Account and game data are stored with Google Firebase, which processes it on our behalf: Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (accounts and friends lists) and Firebase Realtime Database in Europe (Belgium, europe-west1) for live games and the quick-play waiting list. Quick-play games are deleted when both players leave. Settings such as card design and sound are saved only on your device.

Deleting your data

You can ask for your account and all related data to be deleted at any time through the contact link on pishpirik26.github.io/pishpirik.

Children

The game is rated 12+. It is not directed at children under 12.

Politika e privatësisë (shqip)

Loja me CPU nuk kërkon llogari dhe nuk mbledh të dhëna personale. Për lojën online ruhen vetëm: emri i përdoruesit dhe fjalëkalimi (te Google Firebase, ne s'e shohim fjalëkalimin), lista e shokëve, ftesat, gjendja dhe rezultatet e lojërave, si dhe mesazhet e chat-it gjatë lojës. Te "Luaj me dikë" (pa llogari) Firebase të jep një ID anonime; kundërshtari sheh vetëm një emër të gjeneruar (p.sh. "Lojtar 4F2K") dhe nëse luan në telefon, kompjuter apo TV. Lojërat live ruhen në Firebase Realtime Database në Evropë (Belgjikë) dhe lojërat e shpejta fshihen kur dalin të dy lojtarët. Nuk mbledhim emër real, email, vendndodhje apo ID reklamash. S'ka reklama dhe s'ka blerje. Të dhënat përdoren vetëm që loja të funksionojë dhe nuk u jepen të tjerëve. Mund të kërkosh fshirjen e llogarisë në çdo kohë.
